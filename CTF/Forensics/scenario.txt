SOC TICKET #4471 — Possible data exfiltration
Priority: High
Reported by: NDR sensor (behavioral alert)

Summary:
Our network detection sensor flagged workstation 10.0.0.37 (finance VLAN) for
an unusual, sustained pattern of outbound DNS lookups over a short window. The
volume and the shape of the domain names don't match normal browsing. We suspect
a host on this segment is quietly pushing data OFF the network.

We pulled a packet capture from the gateway span port covering the alert window
(capture.pcap, attached). Most of it looks like ordinary user traffic.

Your task:
  - Determine WHAT left the network during this window.
  - Recover the exact data that was exfiltrated.

Notes:
  - The capture is a small sample; everything you need is inside it.
  - Assume the endpoint is compromised. Focus on what it is sending, and where.

Deliverable: the recovered data, verbatim.
